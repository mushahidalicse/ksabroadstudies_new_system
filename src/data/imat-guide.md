# IMAT & Universitaly guide (non-EU / Pakistan-focused)

Factual overview for English-taught single-cycle Medicine, Dentistry, and Veterinary degrees at Italian public universities. **Dates and seat numbers change every year — confirm on MUR and Universitaly before you act.**

## What IMAT is

The **International Medical Admissions Test (IMAT)** is Italy’s national English admission test for:

- Medicine and Surgery (LM-41)
- Dentistry and Dental Prosthodontics (LM-46)
- Veterinary Medicine (LM-42)

For **A.Y. 2026/2027** the rules are in **MUR Decreto Ministeriale n. 1005 of 6 August 2026**. The paper is prepared by **CINECA** for MUR (older pages that still say “Cambridge Assessment” are outdated for this cycle).

IMAT is **not** used for Italian-taught Medicine (semestre filtro) or for English Pharmacy (e.g. Bologna–Rimini uses a separate university selection such as CEnT-S).

Official decree page: [DM n. 1005 / 06-08-2026](https://www.mur.gov.it/it/atti-e-normativa/decreto-ministeriale-n-1005-del-06-08-2026)

## Test format (2026/27)

- **60** multiple-choice questions, **5** options, **100** minutes, max **90** points
- Scoring: **+1.5** correct, **−0.4** wrong, **0** blank
- Sections: reading skills & knowledge from studies (4); logical reasoning & problems (5); biology (23); chemistry (15); physics & mathematics (13)

Syllabus: Allegato A of DM 1005.

## Medicine/Dentistry vs Veterinary

At registration you choose **one** track (irrevocable after the deadline):

1. **Medicine and Dentistry** (shared test and EU ranking logic; you can list both kinds of programmes in preference order), **or**
2. **Veterinary Medicine** (separate ranking)

## Non-EU students residing abroad (including Pakistan)

1. **Eligibility** — Secondary-school diploma meeting Italian university entry rules for foreign qualifications (12 years of schooling or equivalent pathway). Check CIMEA / declaration of value / university instructions for Pakistan HSSC/A-Levels.
2. **Universitaly pre-enrolment** — Create an account on [Universitaly](https://www.universitaly.it/) and complete **pre-enrolment for international students** for your chosen university after (or as required alongside) the IMAT process: [Studenti stranieri](https://www.universitaly.it/studenti-stranieri). The university must validate the pre-enrolment.
3. **IMAT registration** — In the IMAT window, register on Universitaly, pay the test fee, pick the Medicine/Dentistry **or** Veterinary track, and list university preferences. Non-EU abroad compete for **reserved local seats** at the university of pre-enrolment / preference rules stated in the decree and each *bando*.
4. **Sit the test** — In Italy at a participating university venue, or in a foreign centre listed in Tabella A for that year (Pakistan is **not** automatically a centre every year — check Tabella A / rectification notices).
5. **After scores** — Follow the **local non-EU ranking** of your university (not the EU national scrolling list). If assigned, enrol on the university portal within their deadlines.
6. **Study visa** — After university validation on Universitaly, apply for a **type-D study visa** at the Italian Embassy/Consulate competent for your place of residence in Pakistan, with passport, admission/enrolment proof, financial means, accommodation, insurance, and other documents they list. **Consular checklists change — follow the Embassy site, not blogs.**

## Documents often requested (confirm per university + Embassy)

- Passport and photos  
- Secondary diploma + transcript; official translation / legalisation or Apostille as required  
- Universitaly pre-enrolment summary  
- IMAT registration / score evidence if requested  
- Proof of funds and accommodation for the visa  
- Health insurance covering the stay  

English certificates are **not** required to sit IMAT; some universities may ask for English level later for enrolment or clinical years. Italian becomes important for patient contact in later years even on English courses.

## Practical tips

- Prefer **university .it / Universitaly / MUR** pages over consultants’ blogs.  
- Pay only official IMAT / university fees on Universitaly or the university portal.  
- Keep screenshots of every Universitaly confirmation.  
- For the next intake after 2026/27, wait for the new DM — do not reuse this year’s dates blindly.
